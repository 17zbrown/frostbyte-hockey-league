Make a contract offerAccepting is the signing — no league-office step (Rule 2.2)

Free agency is a straight negotiation between a club and a player. Management sends terms; he accepts, counters, or declines. The moment he accepts, he is on the roster.

After you send

Your offers, tracked

The board row doesn’t change when you send. Refresh the page, then open your Dashboard — every offer you have out lives on one card.

5

Find it on the Dashboard

The card Contract offers you have out lists each one. Waiting on him means it’s his move — one button, Withdraw, which closes the offer (he is told).

6

Answer his counter

When he counters, the chip flips to His number — your move and you get three: Walk away, Revise, or Accept his terms. Accepting signs him at his number and he joins the roster at once. Rule 2.2

Your own players

Re-sign before he walks

A player in the final season of his deal is yours to keep — an exclusive right no other club can touch until free agency opens (Rule 2.2).

7

Extend from the Roster

On Team HQ → Roster, a final-season row shows a Final season chip and an Extend button. Its status gains an Offer out chip once you send.

#/hub/roster

8

It’s a next-season deal

The modal prefills his current number and is checked against next season’s cap — nothing about this season changes. A new offer replaces any live one. Rule 2.5

For the player

An offer lands on your dashboard

There is no badge for it. The offer sits at the top of your My Hub dashboard, and accepting is the signing — right there, no waiting on the league.

9

Read the offer

Open My Hub → Dashboard. The card Contract offer for you (1 waiting) shows the club, the money, the term, when you’d join, and the club’s note.

10

Accept, counter, or decline

Accept and sign puts you on their roster immediately — no league approval — and withdraws every other offer you hold. Counter sends your own number and the offer leaves the card until they answer; Decline passes. Rule 2.2

For the player

Final season? Open with your own number

In the last year of your deal you don’t have to wait to be asked. Only your own club can talk to you now — anyone else is tampering (Rule 2.2).

11

Ask to re-sign

The Your contract card carries a Final season chip and an Ask to re-sign button. Name your Salary you want and Term, then Send my ask. It goes to your club as your opening number for a deal starting next season, checked against the cap they’ll have then. They can accept it, come back with their own, or walk away. Rule 2.5

Watch outAccepting is final — the player is on the roster the instant he accepts, every other offer he holds is withdrawn, and there is no undo except waivers. Only one live negotiation exists per club and player: a new offer to the same player replaces your previous one. Cap space, roster room, and the window are re-checked at acceptance, so an offer that fit when you sent it can still be refused with the server’s message.
Good to knowUnder an Owner approves policy, a GM’s or AGM’s offer, revision, answer, or extension is queued for the Owner — the toast reads “Sent to the Owner for approval …” and nothing has reached the player yet. The Owner decides on #/hub/management (Rule 2.6). Cap figures and contract values stay confidential team information (Rule 2.3).
