Waive a playerImmediate and final. He leaves your roster, his cap hit clears, and any club may sign him at the league minimum.

One button on the Roster page, one confirm. Then the player sits on the Waived players board until a club with room signs him — or until the movement deadline closes it.

Where it lives

The Roster page, one row at a time

Every player you may move has four buttons on his row. The management seats have none — they cannot be waived, traded or blocked.

From any hub page, open the Team HQ group in the left sidebar and pick Roster. The page is confidential to your front office: salaries, cap space and block status live here.

The waiver

Confirm, and he’s gone

The confirm dialog says exactly what happens. There is no league-office step and no undo.

3

Read the confirm, then Waive player

The dialog reads: “They come off your roster immediately, their cap hit clears, and any club with room in their position group can sign them at the league minimum until the movement deadline (Rule 2.2). The move is logged for the whole league.” Press Waive player.

4

What changes, right away

The toast reads “‹player› waived — any club may sign him now.” The Squads card drops to 14 pro, the row is gone, and your cap space grows by his full salary. The transaction log posts the move to the league on its own.

Signing a waived player

5

Open Team HQ → Free agents

In this format the page is the Waived players board: every player a club has let go. Approach opens a direct message so you can talk it over first. The Offer button stays grey while your roster is full — waive or trade to make room.

6

Offer — the deal is fixed

Press Offer. The salary reads $750K and cannot be changed; the deal runs to the end of the season. Add a note if you like and press Send offer. He accepts or declines from his own dashboard — the moment he accepts he is on your roster, and the league office confirms nothing. Rule 2.2

For the Owner

When a seat needs sign-off

An Owner can set a GM’s or AGM’s Roster page to Owner approves. Then a waiver queues instead of acting.

7

Queued, then decided

The manager sees a banner — “ItzPeakz approves roster moves” — and his waiver waits under it with Withdraw. The Owner decides at Team HQ → Management → Approvals: Approve runs the waiver on the spot; Deny closes it with a note. Rule 2.6

Watch outWaiving is immediate and final — there is no waiver window, no claim order and no league-office step. The moment you confirm, the player is off your roster and any club may sign him, including the one you play tomorrow. Check the Road to 18 card first: the games he has played stay with him, and if he is short of 18 that is his problem on a new club, not yours.
Good to knowAll movement — waivers and the signing of waived players included — closes at midnight at the end of the Friday of week 4, the movement deadline; after that rosters freeze through the playoffs (Rule 2.4). Moving a player to training camp is not a waiver: he stays yours, his salary still counts, and you can call him back up at any time (Rule 2.1).
