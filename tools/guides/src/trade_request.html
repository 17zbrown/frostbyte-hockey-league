Ask your club for a tradeIt goes to your own front office — not the league office

Want better minutes or a fresh room? File a trade request and it lands with your club’s Owner, GM and AGM. It starts a conversation — the move itself is theirs to make.

Where to file

Start in the Action Center

Everything you send your club or the league office lives here. A trade request is the one type that never leaves your own club.

1

Open the Action Center

Sign in with Discord, open My Hub in the masthead, then choose Action Center in the sidebar.

2

Pick the Trade request card

Four filing cards sit at the top. Choose Trade request — the one that reads “Ask your club’s management for a move — private to your club.” The other three go to the league office.

The form

Say why, then send it

One field, no dropdowns. Tell your management what you want and why — the more concrete you are, the easier it is to act on.

3

Explain the ask

The single field is Why are you requesting a trade? — no subject line, no “who is this about.” Management sees the request without your name attached, so say who you are and what you’re after. The caption reads “Private to your club’s management.”

4

Submit

Press Submit. Leave the box empty and it stops you with “Add details — describe what happened.” Otherwise a green toast reads “Sent to your club’s management” and the modal closes.

After you file

Your case, open and yours to follow

It shows up under your filed cases right away. Add detail or check back any time — replies land in the same thread.

5

Find it under your cases

The list flips to Your filed cases (1) with your new Trade request card, an Open chip and your text. That toast is the confirmation — nothing else fires: no Discord post, no league-office alert for this type.

6

Follow it in the thread

Open the case for its own page — h1 Trade request, back link All cases. Add more in Add a reply or more detail… and press Reply; an Attach a link (optional) field takes an http(s) URL. You’re notified when your management answers.

For the front office

Owner, GM, AGM: reading and answering

There is no inbox for these. Your management opens the case by its link and answers in the thread — the move itself happens later in the Trade Hub.

7

Open it by its link

There is no incoming queue — the Action Center only lists cases you filed. Management reaches a request by its link, and sees the same thread the player does, minus the filer’s name.

8

Answer in the thread

Reply and the player gets a bell alert. The case stays Open — only a commissioner can close it — and the move itself is made club-to-club in the Trade Hub. Rule 2.3

The GM’s view · no ruling buttons, stays Open
Watch outA trade request doesn’t move anyone. It opens a conversation with your own front office — a trade only happens if your management and another club agree to one in the Trade Hub (Rule 2.3), and all player movement freezes at midnight after the Friday of the deadline week (Rule 2.4). You also have to hold a roster spot: a free agent or an unplaced sign-up gets “You need to be on a club roster to request a trade.”
Good to knowThe page’s stock lines — “everything lands with the league office,” “Replies from the league office appear here” — are generic. For a trade request the people reading and answering are your club’s Owner, GM and AGM. It stays private from other clubs and off the league’s Discord and alert feeds, though commissioners and league staff can still see it in their case queue. A rostered Owner, GM or AGM can file one too — but Rule 2.6 shields them from a trade until they leave the seat.
