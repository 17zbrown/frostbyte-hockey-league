The form

Answer every game

Each night lists your club’s three games. Mark each one Available or Not Available — the note covers anything else your GM should know.

3

Answer each game

Every game gets one of two: Available or Not Available. Being out for the 9:40 game does not mark you out for the 9:00 or the 10:20. The counter reads 9/9 games answered when you are done.

4

Add a note where it helps

One note per night — “on at 9:30 after work” tells your GM which game to expect you for. Management reads it in the club grid.

5

Submit

Press Submit availability. Miss a game and nothing saves — you get “Answer all 9 games before submitting.” Mark every game available sets all nine in one tap; you still press Submit.

After you submit

Edit until Wednesday, then it locks

Plans change. The form stays open until the deadline; after that only your GM can work around you.

6

Change your mind any time before the deadline

Come back, change a pill, press Update availability. The chip reads Submitted with the day you logged it; the dashboard offers Review / edit.

7

Wednesday 7:30 PM ET — the window closes

Every pill goes grey and the chip reads Window closed. If you never answered, message your GM: your club sees you as no response, which is worse than any honest answer. Rule 5.1

Watch outIt is one answer per game — nine a week — so answer all of them, not just the first of each night. There is no weekly quota: mark as many or as few games as you can honestly play. But the playoffs have a floor — you need 16 regular-season games to be dressed in them (Season 1's published floor, Rule 8.3) — and a game you never made yourself available for is a game you did not play. Skip the form entirely for weeks on end and the league office reviews it (Rule 5.1).
Good to knowThe window opens once the previous week's last game is final — in practice, answer over the weekend and no later than Wednesday 7:30 PM ET, 90 minutes before the first puck drop. Late answers go to your GM directly and are recorded as late. The most anyone plays is 6 games a week (camp players 3), so “Available” for all nine still means your GM picks your six (Rule 5.2). Your answers are private to your own club's management and league staff.
