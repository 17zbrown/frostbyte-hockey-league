The week opens once the schedule is posted. Until you answer, the dashboard and the sidebar both say so.

The form

Answer every night

One pill covers all three games that night. Only Unavailable keeps you out of a lineup; the other answers tell your GM what to plan around.

3

Tap one pill per night

Available · Unavailable · Maybe · Available late · Available until… · Emergency sub only. The counter reads 3/3 nights answered when you are done.

4

Add a note where it helps

“on at 9:30 after work” is worth more than a Maybe. Management reads the note in the club grid.

5

Submit

Press Submit availability. Miss a night and nothing saves — you get “Answer all 3 nights before submitting.” Copy last week (all available) marks every night Available in one tap; you still press Submit.

After you submit

Edit until Wednesday, then it locks

Plans change. The form stays open until the deadline; after that only your GM can work around you.

6

Change your mind any time before the deadline

Come back, change a pill, press Update availability. The chip reads Submitted with the day you logged it; the dashboard offers Review / edit.

7

Wednesday 7:30 PM ET — the window closes

Every pill goes grey and the chip reads Window closed. If you never answered, message your GM: your club sees you as no response, which is worse than any honest answer. Rule 5.1

Watch outIt is one answer per night, not per game — the three games on a night share it. And the floor is real: mark yourself available for at least six of the week's nine games, including all three games on at least two nights (Rule 5.1). Habitually less and the league office reviews it.
Good to knowThe window opens once the previous week's last game is final — in practice, answer over the weekend and no later than Wednesday 7:30 PM ET, 90 minutes before the first puck drop. Late answers go to your GM directly and are recorded as late. Your answers are private to your own club's management and league staff.
