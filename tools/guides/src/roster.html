Where it lives

Open your cap sheet

Roster sits in Team HQ. It opens on your money — payroll, cap space, and where every deal lands across four seasons.

1

Open Team HQ → Roster

From any hub page, open the Team HQ group in the left sidebar and pick Roster. The page leads management-only: a red note keeps salaries, cap space, and trade-block status between your Owner, GM, and AGM. Rule 2.3

2

Read the three cap tiles

Active payroll · Cap space · Salary cap. Cap space is green when you're under and red when you're over. Every salary counts — training-camp deals included (Rule 2.5).

3

Plan with the cap outlook

Cap outlook lays out this season and the next three. A deal that starts next season is checked against next season's space, not the room you have now — contracts turn over when free agency opens. Rule 2.5

Your squad

Move between roster and camp

Nine forwards, six defensemen, two goaltenders — plus up to three in camp. Squad changes are unlimited all season.

4

Read the Squads card

The active roster is 9 forwards (any mix of C/LW/RW), 6 defensemen (either side), and 2 goaltenders — goalie is the one role locked exactly. Training camp holds up to 3 more; a meter over its cap turns red. Rule 2.1

5

Send down or call up

To camp moves an active player down; Call up brings a camp player onto the roster. A camp player carries a Camp chip, and you can move players either way as often as you like all season.

6

Full? Swap instead

When camp is full at 3, or the position group is full on the active roster, the button becomes Swap…. Pick a same-group counterpart — a wing for a center is fine; a goaltender only for a goaltender. The toast reads Squads swapped.

Moving a player on

Block, trade, or waive

Every skater's row carries its own actions. Management rows are protected — no waive, trade, or block.

7

List on the block, or open a trade

To block flips a player's status to On block and the card counts how many you've listed; Off block takes him back off. Trade opens the Trade Hub with him already in your outgoing package.

8

Waive a player

Press Waive, then confirm with Waive player. He comes off your roster at once, his cap hit clears, and he returns to the free-agent pool for any club to sign (Rule 2.2). Owner, GM, and AGM carry management contracts and can't be waived, traded, or blocked. Rule 2.6

In the pre-season

Loans and Road to 5

Two cards appear only while randomly assigned players are on your club, before the draft.

9

Read the loans block

Randomly assigned players sit in a tinted Pre-season loans block. They aren't your assets — no trades, waivers, or contracts — and they return to the draft pool when the last pre-season game ends (Rule 0.4). RD · reg. C means he's filling a seat away from the position he registered.

10

Watch Road to 5

Each randomly assigned player needs five pre-season appearances to stay draft-eligible, so spread the ice time. A red can't reach 5 means the club has fewer pre-season games left than he needs. Rule 2.8

For the front office

When the Owner signs off

The Owner can set any Team HQ page per seat: full access, Owner approves, or hidden. Under Owner approves, nothing on this page happens until the Owner says so.

11

GM & AGM: your move may need a sign-off

If your Owner set your seat to Owner approves, every button here queues the move instead of making it — the toast reads Sent to the Owner for approval. A banner lists what's Waiting; Withdraw pulls one back before it's decided. Rule 2.6

12

Owner: approve or deny

Queued moves land on Team HQ → Management under Approvals. Approve makes the move through the league's usual checks; if it no longer fits, you both see why (could not be made). Deny tells your manager, with an optional note.
