Build a line

Click a player, then a slot

Click a roster card, then the slot — or drag one onto another to swap. Groups bind: forwards fill the wings and center, defensemen the blue line, the goalie the crease.

3

Click a player, then a slot

Click a card and the message reads “Now click the slot for <player> — any line, any matching position.” Click the slot to fill it; drag a filled slot onto another to swap, or back onto the roster to clear. Then press Save changes — a toast reads “1 line saved.”

Dress it

Saving a line dresses nobody

A saved line is a plan. A game gets a lineup only when you dress it — and that is where the league’s checks run: weekly caps, suspensions, and each game’s 30-minute lock.

5

Point each game night at a line

In the Night plan, use each night’s Dresses picker. A toast confirms it — “Saturday dresses Energy.” Empty lines stay disabled until you fill them.

6

Dress the night, or the whole week

Dress 1 game files that night; Dress the week (2) files them all. Confirm, and each dressing runs the checks — anything refused is reported by night and the rest still land. Toast: “Week dressed — 2 games.” Rule 5.2

Per-game

One game, one lineup

The per-game page (unlisted) adjusts a single night. Reach it from the builder’s links or the game chips. Fill it from your plan, auto-fill, or set it by hand.

7

Open the game and fill it

Each game has its own chip, so games that lock at different times are reachable on their own. Fill from Top Six drops in the line the plan points at, Auto-fill best available takes each player at his own position first, and Clear empties it.

8

Submit the six

All six slots are required — miss one and you get “Fill every slot first — missing …” Press Submit lineup: your starters go to the league office and release to the opponent at the lock. You can resubmit until then.

After the lock

Emergency call-up only

Each game locks 30 minutes before puck drop. After that only management can change it, and only as an emergency call-up — every player swapped costs the club one in-game penalty.

9

The game locks at puck drop minus 30

The bar turns to “Status: Locked · locked 8:30 PM ET (Rule 5.3).” Every slot reads OVR · locked, and the only control left is Emergency call-up. Rule 5.3

10

Swap the no-show, then submit

Enable the call-up, change the one player, and press Submit emergency call-up. Each player changed costs the club one in-game penalty, served in that game — and the opponent already sees your locked sheet. Change only who you must.

For the front office

The Owner decides who can touch it

The Owner sets each seat’s access to this page: full, Owner approves, or hidden. The Owner’s own moves never wait.

11

Owner approves, or hidden

Under Owner approves, every save, night-plan change and dressing is queued to the Owner — a banner says so, and you can Withdraw a waiting move. Set to hidden, the page and its nav item disappear for that seat. Rule 2.6

For the players

You just check you’re in

A rostered player builds nothing. The site tells you only when you ARE in a confirmed lineup — a scratch is never announced, so check your dashboard.

12

Tonight’s slate, then the matchup

Your dashboard’s Tonight’s slate marks your game. When a real lineup includes you it says “You’re in the confirmed lineup at Left Wing.” The matchup page keeps both sheets sealed until the lock, then releases them together. Rule 5.3

Watch outAfter the lock the opponent already sees your sheet. An emergency call-up costs one in-game penalty per player changed, per submission — swap two and that is two penalties (Rule 5.3). With several games on a night, each locks on its own puck drop: games two and three can lock before game one is done, so reach them by their own chip.
Good to knowWeekly caps — three games for a skater, six for a goalie — are checked when a lineup is dressed, not when a line is saved, so a player can sit on all three lines. Server picks and lobby codes are not here; they live on the Schedule desk and the matchup page. In the pre-season there are no caps and your Owner, GM and AGM may dress at any position (Rules 0.4, 5.2).
