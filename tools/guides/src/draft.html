Where it lives

The war room

The Draft desk lives in Team HQ. It builds your board before the night and runs your picks during it — only your club's Owner, GM and AGM can open it.

1

Open the Draft desk

Sign in with Discord, then open Draft at the bottom of the Team HQ group in the hub sidebar. The desk opens under Draft desk with your board, the pool, and the pick-by-pick table.

#/hub/draft

2

Read the status strip

Five tiles run across the top: draft status, the pick clock, who is on the clock, your next pick, and how many players are on your board. Before draft night the status reads Board set — not started and the clock shows dashes; it flips to LIVE when the draft opens.

Before the night

Rank your board

Boards unlock when the pre-season opens. Add players and drag them into order — there is no Save button; every change saves on its own.

3

Boards unlock with the pre-season

Until the pre-season opens the board is LOCKED. Once it does, the registered player pool appears here — watch the games, then rank your targets. The board is private to your club: only your management and the league office ever see it.

4

Search the pool, then Add

Type a gamertag in Search the pool… or filter by position — ALL C LW RW LD RD G. Press Add and the player drops onto the bottom of your board.

5

Rank with the arrows

Move a target up, down, or straight to #1, or × to drop him. Rows renumber at once and each change saves — there is no Save button. Only a failure speaks up: “The board didn’t save…”, and the board re-syncs.

6

Watch Board coverage

The Board coverage chip grades your list against your remaining picks. Covered (green) means three names to spare; short (red) means that if your clock runs out past your board, the league picks best-available overall — so rank more. Players who aren't draft-eligible yet don't count. Rule 2.8

Draft night

On the clock

When your turn comes the on-the-clock tile turns red and you get 120 seconds. Take your top target in one click, or open the pool and choose anyone.

7

Draft your top target in one click

The amber card names your highest-ranked player still available: Draft [name] (board #1). One click and it's done — “[name] drafted — welcome aboard.”

8

Or choose anyone from the pool

Press Choose from the pool for the Draft a player window. Eligible prospects list best-overall-first; type in Start typing a gamertag… to narrow it, then press Draft.

9

Miss the clock? The board picks for you

Each club gets 120 seconds. Run the clock to 0:00 and the league auto-drafts your top available board player — never someone you didn't rank, unless your board runs dry. Keep one tab open: the desk re-reads the draft every ten seconds, so you never need to refresh.

10

A skipped pick is still yours

A pick your clock ran past isn't lost. It waits under Make-up picks, and Use this pick opens the same pool window for it, any time before the draft ends.

All night

Trade the picks

Picks are club assets. Trade them before and during the draft from the Trade Hub — a traded pick, even the one on the clock, changes hands the moment the other side accepts.

11

Trade picks from the Trade Hub

In the Trade Hub, build an offer with Add player / pick → Draft picks, where each round is a selectable asset (’1 R1 pick). Send it, or take an incoming one with Accept — “Accept this trade?” confirms it and it must clear your cap. A used pick can't be traded; a skipped make-up pick can. Rule 2.3

For everyone

The draft room

Every member and guest watches the same picks land live in the draft room — no management access needed.

12

Watch it live at the draft room

Open The draft room for the live clock, the Latest picks ticker, and the full board. Each pick shows its round price — $4M · set by round for a first-rounder, down to the $750K minimum in round 14. Spectators never see the prospect pool: what you see is what the league sees. Managers get a Draft a player button here too when their club is up.

Watch outThe draft can't start until every club has an Owner, a GM and an AGM (Rule 2.6) and every pre-season loan is released — the league office seats and releases these, not you. A loaned player needs five pre-season appearances to be draft-eligible, so spread the ice time: watch Road to 5 on your Roster page. Ranking an ineligible player is allowed but useless — the one-click button skips him and the auto-pick refuses him.
Good to knowPicks pay by round: a first-rounder signs at $4M, each round down is $250K less, to the $750K minimum in round 14. If your Owner set Draft to Owner approves, your board saves and picks go to them first; set to hidden, you won't see the desk at all (Rule 2.6). Skipped picks stay usable until the commissioner concludes the draft; ten minutes later, everyone still unpicked is placed on a club at random.
