Where it lives

The war room

The Draft desk lives in Team HQ. It builds your board before draft day and runs your picks during it. The public draft room is for everyone else.

Before draft day

Rank your board

Every player who registers is in the pool; the sign-up cutoff — 11:59 PM ET on the Thursday before the draft — closes it. Add players and drag them into order. There is no wrong answer, only an empty one.

3

The board unlocks with the pool

Until sign-ups come in the board is Locked. As the pool fills, My draft board opens — private to your club, and never seen by another front office.

4

Search, filter, Add

Type a gamertag in Search the pool or tap a position filter — C · LW · RW · LD · RD · G — then press Add. He lands at the bottom of your list.

5

Order it

The arrows move a player up, down or straight to #1; ✕ drops him. Your top available player is the one the one-click pick offers you on draft day — and the one the league takes for you if your clock runs out.

6

Watch Board coverage

The Board coverage chip grades your list against your remaining picks. Short (amber) means you have fewer names than picks — the league would draft best-available overall once your board runs dry. Covered (green) means you are set for the whole draft.

The order

Drawn at random, then it snakes

The commissioner draws the order before draft day and publishes it. Odd rounds run in the drawn order, even rounds in reverse — pick last in round one, pick first in round two.

The draft, pick by pick · your rows are tinted
7

Find your rows

The draft, pick by pick lists every pick of the draft with your club’s rows tinted — so you can see exactly when you are up. The number of rounds is a season setting the commissioner publishes before the draft; the desk is built to it. Picks cannot be traded this season, so the order you see is the order the draft runs. Rules 2.3 · 2.8

Draft day · 9:00 PM ET

On the clock

Every club gets 120 seconds a pick. When the strip turns red, it is you.

8

One click from your board

Your tile turns red and a bar reads “You’re on the clock.” Press Draft ‹your #1› (board #1) and he is yours — the toast confirms it and the clock passes to the next club.

9

Or choose from the pool

Choose from the pool opens the whole pool with a search box. Press Draft beside any name. Players already taken are struck off your board live — GONE · UTA — so you never draft a ghost.

10

Miss the clock? The pick is skipped

Run the clock to 0:00 and nobody drafts for you: the pick is skipped and the room moves on. You can still use a skipped pick any time before the commissioner concludes the draft (make-up pick, right here). A pick you never use is replaced after the draft by one random player placed on your roster at the league minimum (Rule 2.8).

11

A skipped pick is still yours

If a pick has to be skipped, it waits under Make-up picks. Use this pick opens the pool for it any time before the draft ends.

Watch it live

Open The draft room for the live clock, the club on the clock, the Latest picks ticker and the full board. Players see their own name land the moment it is called; the same pick posts to #draft-hub. Once the draft concludes, anyone still undrafted is placed on a club as depth ten minutes later — nobody is left out. Rule 2.8

The numbers

What a pick costs

Every drafted player signs a one-season deal at a salary fixed by his round. The first round pays the most; each round down is $250K less, to the $750K minimum in the last round.

120seconds on the clock, every pick
15active-roster spots to fill — two full lines plus three of any position (Rule 2.1)
1season on every contract — everyone re-enters next year’s draft (Rule 2.5)
$750Kthe league minimum, and the pay of the last round and every depth placement
Watch outThe draft can’t start until every club has an Owner and a GM (Rule 2.8); an open AGM seat can be filled after. The league office seats an empty post first. Draft to the shape: at most 9 forwards, 7 defensemen or 5 goaltenders fit on a 15-man roster, and the desk refuses a pick that would break it. And bring a full board: a short board plus a dropped connection is how you end up with three goalies.
Good to knowYour board is never sent to another club, and the auto-pick reads it exactly as you ordered it — so order matters more than length. If your Owner set Draft to Owner approves, board saves queue for approval; on draft day, picks themselves are always immediate (Rule 2.6). Puck drop is the Wednesday after the draft — your first lineups are due that afternoon.
