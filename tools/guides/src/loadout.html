Builds

Locked to your position

Also out

Loadouts and cosmetics

The league is played on standard created players, built by hand.

Pre-built loadouts

Every pre-built loadout is banned

The game’s ready-made builds may not be dressed. Build your player yourself, on a legal build for your position.

Special characters

Special-character classes and cosmetics are banned

No special or mascot character may be dressed, and none of their cosmetics may be worn. Standard created players only, at every position.

If you load in with one

What happens

Responsibility sits with the player and his club’s management. The lineup tools cannot see your build — you have to.

The game is restarted

Load into a game with a banned ability, build, loadout or cosmetic and the game does not count as loaded — either club calls it, and it restarts.

You take a penalty

The offending player serves one minor penalty at the start of the restarted game. Refuse to restart and your club forfeits (Rule 3.2).

Check before the lock

Managers confirm every dressed player’s build and abilities before lineups lock, 30 minutes before puck drop (Rule 5.3). Repeat offenders are reviewed under Chapter 7.

Watch outThe list is written against NHL 27 and the league office may add to it as the game is patched or the meta shifts. Any change is posted with that week’s schedule and in the changelog — the rulebook’s Rule 4.5 is always the definitive list.
