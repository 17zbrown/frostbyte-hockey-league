League rules · Rule 4.5

Banned X‑FactorsSkill, not loadout. Six abilities are out at every tier; two more are out at Red tier.

Dress a banned ability, a pre-built loadout or a special-character cosmetic and the lineup is ineligible — the game is a forfeit. Check your build before every lock.

Also out

Loadouts and cosmetics

The league is played on standard created players, built by hand and locked to their position group.

Pre-built loadouts

Every pre-built loadout is banned

The game’s ready-made builds may not be dressed. Build your player yourself, on a legal build for your position — a forward on a forward build, a defenseman on a defense build.

Special characters

Special-character classes and cosmetics are banned

No special or mascot character may be dressed, and none of their cosmetics may be worn. Standard created players only, at every position.

If you dress one

What happens

Responsibility sits with the player and his club’s management. The lineup tools cannot see your build — you have to.

The game is forfeit

A banned ability, loadout or cosmetic makes the lineup ineligible under Rule 5.2 — the game is lost where it stands.

Discipline can follow

A forfeit is without prejudice to further discipline under Chapter 7, for the player and for the club that iced him.

Check before the lock

Managers confirm every dressed player’s build and abilities before lineups lock, 30 minutes before puck drop (Rule 5.3).

Watch outThe list is written against NHL 27 and the league office may add to it as the game is patched or the meta shifts. Any change is posted with that week’s schedule and in the changelog — the rulebook’s Rule 4.5 is always the definitive list.
Good to knowBuilds are locked to their position group: a forward may not use the Enforcer build or any defenseman build; a defenseman may not use the Enforcer Defenseman build or any forward build. The full legal set is in Rule 4.5.
