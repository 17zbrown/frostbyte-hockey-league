Offer players and picks, weigh what other clubs send you, and close the deal — all in the Trade Hub. The league has no say in the deal itself: the cap and roster checks run the instant it’s accepted.

Send an offer

Build a trade, then propose it

Pick a club, put pieces on both sides, and send it. There is no confirm on Propose and no cap preview — so check both columns first.

3

Pick the trade partner

Choose a club in Trade partner. The right column becomes ‹their name› send and its Add player / pick switches on. Changing the partner clears the other side.

4

Add players and picks

Press Add player / pick under a column, choose from Players and Draft picks, then press Done. Owner, GM, and AGM never appear — management contracts can’t be traded. Rule 2.6

Shortcut: the Trade button on your Roster page opens this builder with that player already in your send column.

5

Write a note, then Propose

Add a line on why it works, then press Propose to VAN — the button carries the club’s code. It lands in Offers you’ve sent with a Proposed chip; pull it back any time with Withdraw offer before they answer.

When an offer comes in

Counter, decline, or accept

Every incoming offer carries three buttons. Only Accept moves players — and it is immediate.

6

Counter

Press Counter to load their offer, mirrored, into your builder — what they asked for goes in your column, what they offered in theirs. Adjust it and Propose; that quietly declines the original. Counter replaces whatever you were building.

7

Decline

Press Decline to send it back. No confirm — the toast reads “Offer declined” and the other club is told.

8

Accept

Press Accept, then confirm. “The players and picks change hands immediately and it’s logged.” You get “Trade completed — rosters updated for both clubs” and both cap sheets update. No league sign-off; only the transactions department can reverse it. Rule 2.3

The trade block

Advertise a player — on the Roster page

The block isn’t set in the Trade Hub. You list players on Roster, and a listing is announced in your club’s #trade-block channel.

9

List with To block

On a player’s row press To block to advertise him; the status flips to On block and the toast reads “Frostbyte listed on the trade block.” Off block takes him back off.

10

Management is protected

Owner, GM, and AGM rows read “Management contract — protected” — no block, trade, or waive. A pre-season loan can’t be traded either. Rule 2.6

For the Owner

When a seat needs sign-off

An Owner can set a GM’s or AGM’s Trade Hub to Owner approves. Then every button on the page queues instead of acting.

11

The manager’s view

A banner sits on the page — “ItzPeakz approves trade hub moves.” Each button toasts “Sent to the Owner for approval…” and the move waits; the manager can pull it back with Withdraw.

12

The Owner decides

The Owner reviews at Team HQ → Management → Approvals: Approve or Deny each move. Approve and make it runs it through the usual cap and roster checks at once. Rule 2.6

Watch outAccept is final and immediate — there is no league-office approval step, and the builder shows no cap preview. The cap and roster checks run when the other club accepts, so a clean-looking offer can bounce at acceptance with “Couldn’t accept: …”. Only the transactions department can reverse a completed trade, and only before a piece in it moves again (Rule 2.3).
Good to knowAll movement — trades included — closes at midnight ending the Friday of the deadline week; after that rosters freeze through the playoffs (Rule 2.4). The picker offers only the current season’s unused draft picks, and a signed extension travels with its player and counts against his new club (Rule 2.5).
