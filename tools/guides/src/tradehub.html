Where it lives

Get in — it’s management-only

Two front offices meet here. The sidebar badge counts offers waiting on you, and everything on the page is private to your club’s Owner, GM and AGM.

In the hub sidebar, under Team HQ, open Trade Hub — on a phone, swipe the rail of chips under the top bar until you reach it. A yellow badge there is the number of offers other clubs have sent you.

Send an offer

Build a trade, then propose it

Pick a club, put players on both sides, and send it. There is no confirm on Propose and no cap preview — so check both columns first.

3

Pick the trade partner

Choose a club in Trade partner. The right column becomes ‹their name› send and its Add player switches on.

4

Add players to both sides

Press Add player under a column and pick from the list — your own roster on the left, theirs on the right. Management never appears: Owner, GM and AGM are protected. Draft picks are not trade assets this season, so the picker is players only. Rule 2.3

5

Write a note, then Propose

Add a line on why it works, then press Propose to SEA — the button carries the club’s code. The toast reads “Trade proposed to Kraken” and it lands in Offers you’ve sent as Proposed.

Changed your mind?

Withdraw offer pulls it back any time before they accept.

When an offer comes in

Counter, decline, or accept

Every incoming offer carries three buttons. Only Accept moves players — and it is immediate.

7

Counter

Press Counter to load their offer, mirrored, into your builder — what they asked for goes in your column, what they offered in theirs. Adjust it and Propose it back.

8

Decline

Press Decline to send it back. No confirm — the toast reads “Offer declined” and the other club is told.

9

Accept

Press Accept, then confirm. “The players change hands immediately and it’s logged.” You get “Trade completed — rosters updated for both clubs”, the transaction log posts itself, and the new player is eligible from your next game. Rule 2.3

The trade block

Advertise a player — on the Roster page

The block isn’t set in the Trade Hub. You list players on Roster, and other front offices see who is available.

10

10

List with To block

On a player’s row press To block to advertise him; the status flips to On block and Off block takes it down. Trade on any row opens the hub with that player already in your column.

11

Management is protected

Owner, GM and AGM rows read “Management contract — protected” — no block, trade or waive. Everyone else, including your camp players and depth placements, can be traded. Rules 2.1 · 2.6

For the Owner

When a seat needs sign-off

An Owner can set a GM’s or AGM’s Trade Hub to Owner approves. Then every button on the page queues instead of acting.

12

The manager’s view

A banner sits on the page — “ItzPeakz approves trade hub moves.” Each button toasts “Sent to the Owner for approval…” and the move waits, with Withdraw beside it. The Owner decides at Team HQ → Management → Approvals. Rule 2.6

Watch outThree games first (Rule 2.4): every player in a trade must have played 3 regular-season games this season; the picker greys out anyone short of it and shows his count. Accept is final and immediate — there is no league-office approval step, and the builder shows no cap preview. The cap and roster checks run when the other club accepts: a deal that would put a club over the $50M cap, or leave it unable to ice a full lineup, is refused right there (Rules 2.1, 2.5). The transactions department can still reverse a trade that isn’t a real hockey deal (Rule 2.3).
Good to knowAll movement — trades, waivers and waived-player signings — closes at midnight at the end of the Friday of week 4, the movement deadline; after that rosters freeze through the playoffs (Rule 2.4). A traded player keeps his one-season deal and his games-played count, so he is as playoff-eligible on his new club as he was on his old one (Rule 8.3).
