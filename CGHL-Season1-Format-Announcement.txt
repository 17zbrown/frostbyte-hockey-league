🏒 **SEASON 1 IS ALMOST HERE — HERE'S THE PLAN**

We're a brand-new league, so we're keeping Season 1 simple: sign up, get drafted, play hockey. No pre-season, no free-agency week, no waiting around. Here's the big picture.

**📅 The dates**
• **Sign up by Thursday, Sept 17 at 11:59 PM ET.** If you're signed up by then, you're in the draft — that's the only requirement.
• **The draft is Saturday, Sept 19 at 9:00 PM ET**, live on the site.
• **First games are Wednesday, Sept 23 at 9:00 PM ET.**
• The season is **6 weeks**, same nights as always — Wednesday, Thursday, Friday.
• Playoffs start **Wednesday, Nov 4**.

**🎯 How it works**
Everyone who signs up on time goes into one big draft, and every team is built from scratch on draft night. Didn't get picked? You're placed on a team right after the draft. Sign up late? You still play — you'll be added to a team. Nobody gets left out.

The season runs 6 weeks, everyone's contract is for this season only, and next season we redraft the whole league and do it all again. Fresh start every time.

**🏆 Playoffs**
6 of the 8 teams make it — the top 3 in each division. Win your division and you get a bye through the first round. Every series is best of 7.

**Still being finalized:** exact roster sizes and the number of draft rounds. We'll post those before draft night.

**The two things to do right now:**
1. If you haven't signed up, do it before Thursday night: chelgamingleague.com/#/register
2. If you have, get in the habit of marking your availability each week — your GM builds the lineups from it.

Questions? Ask in Discord — we're happy to help. See you on the ice.
