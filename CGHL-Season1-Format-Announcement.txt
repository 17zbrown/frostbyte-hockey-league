🏒 **SEASON 1 IS ALMOST HERE — HERE'S HOW IT WORKS**

We're a brand-new league, so we're keeping things simple this season: sign up, get drafted, play hockey. No pre-season, no waiting around. Here's the whole thing in plain English.

**📅 The dates**
• **Sign up by Thursday, Sept 17 at 11:59 PM ET.** If you're signed up by then, you're in the draft — that's the only requirement.
• **Draft night is Saturday, Sept 19 at 7:00 PM ET**, live on the site.
• **First games are Wednesday, Sept 23 at 9:00 PM ET.**
• The season is **6 weeks**, same nights as always — Wednesday, Thursday, Friday.
• Playoffs start **Wednesday, Nov 4**.

**🎯 The draft**
15 rounds, snake style — the order is random, and it flips every round so the team picking last in round one picks first in round two. Didn't get drafted? No problem — you're put on a team right after the draft. Sign up late? You still play; you'll be added to a team as depth.

**👥 Your team**
Each team has 18 players: 9 forwards, 6 defensemen and 3 goalies. **Everyone plays a max of 3 games a week** — goalies too — so every spot on the roster gets ice time. Contracts are for this season only; next season everyone goes back into the draft and we do it all again.

**🏆 Playoffs**
6 of the 8 teams make it — the top 3 in each division. Win your division and you get a bye through the first round. Every series is best of 7.

**The two things to do right now:**
1. If you haven't signed up, do it before Thursday night: chelgamingleague.com/#/register
2. If you have, get in the habit of marking your availability each week — your GM builds the lineups from it.

Full rules are at chelgamingleague.com/#/rulebook. Questions? Ask in Discord — we're happy to help.

See you on the ice. **— The CGHL League Office**
