🏒 **SEASON 1 — THE NEW FORMAT**

We're a new league, so we're running Season 1 lean, fast and focused on getting everyone on the ice. Here's what changed, effective now:

**📅 The calendar**
• Sign-ups close **Thursday Sept 17, 11:59 PM ET** — everyone registered by then is in the draft
• **Draft night: Saturday Sept 19, 7:00 PM ET**, live on the site
• **Puck drop: Wednesday Sept 23, 9:00 PM ET**
• 6-week regular season, 9 games a week — same Wed/Thu/Fri nights and times
• Trade deadline after week 4 · Playoffs from Wednesday Nov 4

**🎯 The draft**
15 rounds, snake order, drawn at random. Round 15 pays $750K, round 1 $4.25M. No pre-season and no game requirement — if you signed up on time, you're in the pool. Anyone not drafted is placed on a club right after the draft, and late sign-ups still play: you're placed as depth until the deadline.

**👥 Rosters**
18 players: 9 forwards, 6 defensemen, 3 goalies — your Owner, GM and AGM are three of the 18. Every player plays at most 3 games a week, goalies included. $50M hard cap. Every contract runs one season, and everyone goes back into the draft next season — no extensions, no held rights. Trades are players for players; picks aren't traded.

**🏆 Playoffs**
Six of eight make it — the top 3 in each division. Division winners rest through the opening round, then the division finals, then the final. Every round a best-of-seven inside one game week.

The rulebook has been rewritten for this format at chelgamingleague.com/#/rulebook — the fuller model we'll grow into is preserved in its Appendix A.

If you haven't signed up: you have until Thursday night. If you have: build your availability habit now — your GM sets lineups from it starting week 1.
