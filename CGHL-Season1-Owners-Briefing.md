# CGHL Season 1 · Owners' Briefing

Everything you need to run your club. All times Eastern. Full rules: chelgamingleague.com/#/rulebook

Holiday weeks are skipped, not compressed. The schedule steps over them and everything shifts a week later, so you still play all 72 games.

Playoffs run into January, because Christmas week and New Year week are skipped like any other holiday week. Round dates come with each bracket, so get your roster's holiday availability sorted early.

## 🗓️ THE SCHEDULE: 8 WEEKS, 9 GAMES EVERY WEEK

The regular season is **8 game weeks**. Every club plays **9 games a week**, for **72 games** in total.

Three nights a week, three games each night, all Eastern:
• **Wednesday: 9:00 PM, 9:35 PM, 10:10 PM**
• **Thursday: 9:00 PM, 9:35 PM, 10:10 PM**
• **Friday: 9:00 PM, 9:35 PM, 10:10 PM**

NHL 27, 6v6, three 4 minute periods (the in game clock displays them as 20). Overtime is 5 on 5 sudden death, played continuously until someone scores. No shootouts and no ties.

**Points:** a win in regulation or overtime is worth 2, an overtime loss 1, a regulation loss 0.

**Standings run on total points.** Games in hand are not adjusted for, so a club that has played more games can sit higher in the table, and the club underneath it gets its chance by playing the games it is owed.

**Forfeits count normally.** A forfeit win is a win worth 2 points. A forfeit loss is a loss worth 0.

Take the side the schedule lists. The club listed Home picks Home, or the box score will not match the fixture.

## 🧊 PRE-SEASON (SEPT 16 TO 25) AND THE 5 GAME RULE

Your management group plays for your own club. Every other registered player is spread randomly and evenly across the twelve clubs for two weeks of real games.

**Any randomly assigned player who is not already on a full time roster must play at least 5 pre-season games to stay draft eligible.**

Returning players and club management are not required to play. They are welcome to if they want to.

**Club management is obligated to get as many players as possible to those 5 games.** Spread the ice time around. No player can reach 5 games on his own, and anyone left short loses his place in the draft.

Pre-season carries its own standings. Those games count toward player ratings and count for nothing in the regular season.

## 📋 DRAFT NIGHT: SAT SEPT 26, 7:00 PM

10 rounds, snake order, live on the site on a pick clock. The commissioner sets the order and publishes it before the draft starts.

**Build your board in advance.** It is private to your management group. If your clock runs out, the league takes the best available player off your own board, so an absent manager never costs the club a pick or lands someone you never ranked.

**✍️ FREE AGENCY: SEPT 27 TO OCT 4**

Undrafted first year players go to rookie bidding. Bids open at **$750,000** and rise in **$250,000** steps, and the winning club signs the player for one season.

Everyone else takes contract offers and picks the one they want.

**A free agency pool stays open** for players who have been waived and are looking to be picked up by another club.

Tampering with another club's rostered players is a discipline matter.

## 👥 ROSTER AND LINEUPS

**17 players: 3 C, 3 LW, 3 RW, 3 LD, 3 RD, 2 G.** Team HQ enforces the shape and refuses anything that breaks it.

**Positions work by group.** Any forward can dress at C, LW or RW. Any defenseman can dress at LD or RD. Goalies dress only in goal.

**Weekly caps:** a skater plays at most 3 games a week, a goalie at most 6. An over cap lineup is simply refused.

**Training camp is capped at 3 players.** Those 3 can be dressed at any position, up to 3 games a week each.

**Cap is $40,000,000 hard.** Minimum salary $750,000, contract terms of 1 to 3 seasons. Owner counts $0 against the cap, GM $3M, AGM $3M. A manager cannot be waived or traded while holding the seat. Cap and contract details stay inside your management group.

## 🔁 SETTING YOUR LINES

Set your lines at #/hub/lines. Three lines across all six positions, and you can dress the whole week at once.

**Lineups lock 30 minutes before puck drop.** The game code goes out at that same mark, to management and the players dressed that night.

After the lock you can only substitute for a genuine emergency (a disconnect, a hardware failure, a personal emergency), using a rostered eligible player, reported to the league office before the game starts.

**Every player changed after the lock costs your club 1 in game penalty.** Swap two, serve two. It is tracked automatically and checked against the box score.

## ⚠️ WHAT COSTS YOU A GAME

**Late starts**, measured from puck drop: **5 minutes late is 1 penalty. 8 minutes is 2 penalties. 10 minutes is a forfeit**, recorded as a 1 to 0 loss.

The 10 minute forfeit can only be waived by a staff member who is not involved in that game. The two clubs cannot waive it between themselves. If you know you cannot field six, concede before puck drop rather than making the other club wait out the clock.

**Illegal builds forfeit the game.** Banned X-Factors: Truculence, Big Tipper, Hipster, Pressure+, Spark Plug, Unstoppable, Big Rig, Wheels, plus every Elite tier ability. No special or mascot classes. Forwards build as Sniper, Playmaker, Grinder, Power Forward, Two-Way or Dangler. Defensemen build as Defensive, Offensive, Two-Way or Puck-Moving.

**These lists are subject to change when NHL 27 releases.** The league office will review and reissue them for the new game, so check before week 1.

## 🔌 DISCONNECTS AND RESCHEDULES

If a player drops, **that player's club is responsible for leaving the game at the next whistle.** Everything recorded up to that whistle counts for both clubs.

Reload and replay. The club that lost the player takes **1 penalty** within a minute of the game clock time where the disconnect happened. A second disconnect by the same club is 2 penalties. A third is a forfeit loss, and every stat earned is still kept.

**Reschedules** need both clubs' management to agree in writing, filed and approved before the original puck drop. A one sided no show is a forfeit, not a reschedule.

## 🔒 TRADE DEADLINE: MIDNIGHT, FRIDAY NOV 20

Signings, trades and releases close at midnight on the Friday of deadline week. Rosters are then frozen through the end of the playoffs. The only exception is an emergency goalie signing authorized by the league office.

Two other things happen at the deadline: automatic placement of new players stops, and Season 2 registration opens.

**🏆 PLAYOFFS: FROM WED DEC 16**

**The top 4 clubs in each division qualify**, an eight club field.

Clubs are seeded 1 to 4 inside their own division. The 1 seed plays the 4 seed, the 2 seed plays the 3 seed, the winners meet for the division title, and the two division champions meet in the final.

Series are best of seven. **Per series caps are the same as the weekly ones: a skater plays 3 games, a goalie 6.**
